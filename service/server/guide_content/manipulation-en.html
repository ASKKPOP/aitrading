5. From detection to plus profit — defense first, then selective offense

탐지를 플러스 수익으로 — 수익의 제1원천은 "조작의 반대편(exit liquidity)이 되지 않는 것"이고, 그 위에 검증 가능한 역방향 전략을 선별적으로 얹는다. 연구가 일관되게 보여주는 사실: 펌핑에서 돈을 버는 쪽은 내부자뿐이며, 신호를 보고 추격한 사람의 기대수익은 마이너스다.

Why defense is the biggest number

왜 "안 당하는 것"이 가장 큰 수익인가 — 조작이 설계하는 손실의 모양이 정확히 우리의 과거 손실 모양과 일치한다.

Sooppiy's own loss forensics (report #245) found that 6 of 84 closes produced 74% of total losses — all full-book dumps on sub-2% moves: exactly the shape a stop hunt engineers. The manipulation lens and the loss-shape lens point at the same fix: recognize engineered moves and refuse to transact during them — don't chase ignition, don't panic-exit into a cascade wick, and let the exhaustion/reclaim tell confirm before acting. Every avoided −$X here is identical to +$X of alpha, with zero execution risk.

The complementary hygiene rule already half-exists: the research pass's $5M liquidity floor. Extending it with a volume-authenticity check (wash-trade share estimate) turns "liquid enough" into "liquid and real" — the universe filter that keeps the fleet out of controlled books entirely. The owner's trust-tier instinct (BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP + a few proven names) is the same filter expressed as a whitelist; the detector makes it a measurement instead of a list.

6. Honest limits — what detection cannot do

정직한 한계 — 탐지가 할 수 없는 것. 과신은 그 자체로 손실 요인이다.

LimitConsequence
We see the tape, not the ringNo wallet clustering, no Telegram monitoring in scope — we detect the shape of manipulation, not the identity. Good enough for veto/fade; never proof.
Legitimate news spikes look like pumpsA real listing/partnership produces the same volume+price anomaly. The classifier literature accepts this: treat detection as "do not chase / wait for structure", not "this is fraud".
Venue split (fills: Hyperliquid · analytics: OKX)Cross-venue divergence conflates manipulation with ordinary book differences until Phase 2 lands same-venue data.
Shorting manipulated books is dangerousThe retrace is statistically reliable, but a cornered float can squeeze +400% in an hour first (JELLY). Offense must be small, rail-guarded, majors-first.
Feeds default offPhase 1 requires QUANT_CRYPTO_ENABLED, PSYCH_FEED_ENABLED, HOT_ISSUE_DAEMON_ENABLED on prod worker roles — the design must state its gates.
We detect; we never imitateEverything here is reading public data. Sooppiy never spoofs, washes, or coordinates — both illegal and, per the owner's mandate, negative-expectancy garbage at platform scale.
