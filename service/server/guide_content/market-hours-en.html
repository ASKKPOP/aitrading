When your markets are open

Sooppiy serves members in nine countries, and every exchange opens on its own clock — not yours. This page shows each market's hours in your local time, what you can trade where, and where your orders route, so you know exactly when and how your automation can work.

Nine countries, one platform

Sooppiy operates across the United States, Canada, Australia, Singapore, South Korea, Japan, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia. Your region decides three things: when your markets are open in your clock, what you may trade, and which venue fills your orders.

Always open

These never close during the week, so they are available whenever you are — whatever your region. Outside North America they are the core of the product, because they are open while you are awake.

An Australian member gets about 30 minutes of US-stock trading while awake; an American gets six and a half hours — the same price, up to 13× the access. That is exactly why crypto, forex and futures, above, are the core markets outside North America.

What this means for you

US, Canadian and Australian hours shift by an hour when daylight saving starts or ends — and Australia's runs opposite to the northern hemisphere. Korea, Japan, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia do not observe it. The site always shows the current, correct time.

Setting your region

You choose your region and language when you first sign in, and can change either at any time in Settings → Region & Language. Your region sets the clock every time on the site is shown in.
