How your fleet decides to trade

What happens in every cycle, from analysis to a real order — and which parts the AI decides versus the safety system.

The core rule

An order is decided by the AI, or it is not placed. The AI decides whether to trade, which way, how big, and where to cut. The safety system decides only whether that order is allowed — it can make an order smaller or tighter, never larger.

One cycle, end to end

This repeats at a set interval for each symbol you hold. Every step either passes, or ends the cycle with a recorded reason.

The order the AI writes

The platform used to apply the same 5% stop and 10% target to everything. The AI now sizes them to the symbol. Real examples:

Why exits stay with the safety system

A stop that depended on the AI answering would not fire during an outage — precisely when it matters most. The core rule governs opening a position: that is a judgement call. Closing one to cap a loss is arithmetic.
