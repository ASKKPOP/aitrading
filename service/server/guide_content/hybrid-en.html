Hybrid Strategy: Chart Patterns × Human Psychology

A buy/sell decision framework that mixes both sides according to what the verified research actually supports: psychology sets the regime (when to trade, how much risk), charts set the trigger (what and at what price), volume + sentiment confirm, and volatility sets the size. No single signal is trusted — you act only on confluence.

CONFLUENCE ENGINE — act only when ≥ 4 of 6 conditions agree

Stacking independent ~53% edges is the only verified way past 55%. One signal alone = no trade. Risk rules (volatility sizing, stops) override everything.

② Decision Flow: The 4-Layer Stack

Every candidate trade passes top to bottom. Any layer can veto.

LAYER 1 Psychology Regime Filter — "Should I trade at all? Which mode?"

VIX < 20 → trend mode (follow charts). VIX 20–30 → half size, tighter stops. VIX > 30 + fear extreme → contrarian mode (ignore bearish patterns, prepare mean-reversion buys). FOMC/CPI/election within ±1 day → no new entries. War/policy shock → never buy the first bounce; wait days 1–3.

▼

LAYER 2 Direction Engine (Charts) — "Long or short bias?"

12-month momentum (skip last month) positive AND price above 200-day MA → long bias. Both negative → short/avoid bias. Mixed → no bias, skip. These are the two most verified predictors in the entire literature.

▼

LAYER 3 Trigger + Confirmation (Charts × Psychology) — "Exactly when?"

Pattern completes (breakout of double bottom, neckline break of H&S) AND volume rises on the break AND overnight/news sentiment does not contradict. Pattern without volume = trap, skip. Chart bullish but sentiment collapsing on real news = psychology vetoes.

▼

LAYER 4 Size & Exit (Risk) — "How much? When out?"

Position size = risk budget ÷ volatility. Hard stop below pattern invalidation. Trail with MA. Time stop: not working in N days → exit. With ~55% accuracy, cutting losers fast IS the edge.
