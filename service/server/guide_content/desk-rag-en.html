2. Collection — how chunks get made

수집 — 청크가 만들어지는 경로. 세 소스 모두 매 트레이딩 틱에 편승해 스로틀·멱등(SHA)·fail-soft로 적재된다. 손상된 코퍼스가 트레이딩 사이클을 죽이는 일은 구조적으로 불가능.

sourcewhat becomes a chunkkindwhy it matters
ingest_decisions() each decision event's report — the desk's stated case — plus the numbers behind it (action, confidence, conviction) decision the most valuable text the platform produces was read once and buried under the next 200 events
ingest_run_outcomes() a terminal run (stopped/finished/failed) joined to its realized result outcome nothing else joins "this decision" to "what it earned or cost" — the desk could never be asked "when you last reasoned like this, what happened?"
ingest_reports() monitoring markdown (handoff, daily cycle notes, backlog) chunked per ## heading — a whole day is too coarse, a paragraph loses its heading monitoring the measured findings (fee hurdle, full-book dumps, ladder churn…) survive account rotation and reach the desk

5. The scoring logic — deliberately boring

스코어링 — 의도적으로 단순하게. 임베딩 서비스·FTS5·MySQL FULLTEXT 없음.

  • Pure-Python lexical scorer. The corpus is small (hundreds to low thousands of chunks); a Python scorer behaves identically on SQLite and MySQL — the dialect split has burned this codebase before (LIKE ESCAPE, datetime functions, FK enforcement).
  • Short stop-word list. Aggressive stop-lists would strip the domain words — loss, stop, close — that queries are actually about.
  • kind=monitoring only is recallable today. The 510 decision and 31 outcome chunks are write-only by design: they are the training data for Phase 3, and injecting them before attribution exists would add noise without a feedback loop.
  • Everything on the cycle path is fail-soft. An empty, slow, or broken corpus returns "" — a cycle is never lost to its own memory.

6. Phase roadmap — where the learning loop is

로드맵 — 순서 규칙이 구속력: 회수에 변동(variance)이 있어야 가중치를 배울 수 있다. 데이터보다 앞서 짓지 않는다. 하루 1증분, 매일 프로드까지.

The overfitting risk is named in the plan: skills and findings are written from measured episodes — that is fitting the past. The mitigation is that every lesson keeps its evidence clause, and weights fall on evidence, never on opinion.

8. What this deliberately is not

이것이 의도적으로 아닌 것 — 흔한 RAG 주장 대비 실제.

common claimhere
"processes vast data instantly"No. The desk sees a small brief. Retrieval sends the right small brief, not more data.
"removes human emotion"Partly. Full-book dumps on sub-2% moves were measured — behaviourally identical to panic. Recall exists to put the measured lesson in front of the next such moment.
"learns automatically"Not yet. Weights require variance in what was retrieved. Journaling that variance is live (#340); learning from it is the daily cadence's job, one guarded increment at a time.
"a vector database"No. A table and a Python scorer, identical on both SQL dialects, fail-soft on the money path. Boring on purpose.
