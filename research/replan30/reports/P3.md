(c) Falsifier: walk-to-stop replay of blocked adds over 30d — if forfeited profit on blocked adds exceeds prevented loss, the cap is net-negative and dies.
(d) Small code.

**6. Book-size / risk cap per run**
(a) Cap total run book notional at 1× the slot's base entry notional (adds cannot grow the book beyond it) and per-trip risk at 0.5% of notional, congruent with R2's stop-cap result (payoff 0.27→1.49 at 0.5%). Params in `order_params.py` rails + `run_engine.py` size clamp. Do NOT raise FLEET_TARGET_SLOTS (known 4–40× sizing amplifier).
(b) Top-5 trips = 66.8% of gross loss, top-10 = 80.8% (R1 finding 4); capping book growth + per-trip risk bounds the dump distribution that carries the dollars.
(c) Falsifier: after 30d/n≥30, if top-5-trip share of gross loss is unchanged (≥60%) the cap didn't bind where losses live.
(d) Small code.

**7. Per-market fee-floor table → market gating**
(a) Script `research/replan30/market_floor.py`: for each of the 5 markets compute RT cost (per-market fee from `fees.py` at FEE_PER_MARKET=true + measured slip) vs that market's median |gross move| and diffusion-at-median-hold from the fills tape. Gate: any market where cost > p50 move at its actual holds gets its desks restricted to ≥12h-horizon theses or paused (crypto already measured failing: 15.8–22.6bp cost vs 22.2bp median move at 1.43h median hold; only 54% of trips out-moved cost — R5 findings 6–7).
(b) Every market is negative (crypto −96.74, us-stock −47.20, futures −22.62, forex −5.09; R1); holds ≥12h are the only net-positive bucket (+5.37, payoff 2.97, R1 finding 6). This is the experiment the owner's rule requires before suspending any market.
(c) Falsifier per market: a market whose table says "cannot clear floor" but whose next-30d net is positive refutes its gate; a "clears floor" market that stays negative refutes the floor as the binding constraint there.
(d) Small code (analysis) + env-level gating on its result — no gate ships before the table exists.
