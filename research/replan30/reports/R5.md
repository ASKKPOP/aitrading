## 2. Does psychology content change decisions? (decisions_sample.tsv, n=300)

Sample = 2026-08-29T15:32Z→08-30T00:50Z (~9.3h, all post-ship). **No contrast group exists**: 300/300 payloads carry the `crypto_psych` brief line and 300/300 carry the `psych_shock` route trigger (a trigger firing 100% of cycles discriminates nothing). Actions: 292 hold / 8 retire / **0 entries** — psychology's effect on entries is unmeasurable in this window; that refutation is the finding.
Within-sample: 27/300 (9%) desk reports explicitly reason from fear/greed/sentiment; all 27 are holds; their mean confidence 0.486 vs 0.521 without mention. Direction consistent with psychology as a *hold/caution* amplifier, not an entry generator — but n and variance are too thin to claim causation. Also: F&G in-window ranged 27–74 (FACTS), so the line's contrarian rule (≤20/≥80) has never had anything to fire on.

## 3. External evidence: sentiment horizon

- Contrarian at extremes, long horizons: sub-10 F&G readings → avg **+48% 90-day** forward return, 0 negative instances; sustained <15 fear → recovery within 6–12 months ([TECHi](https://www.techi.com/bitcoin-fear-and-greed-index-extreme-fear-buying-signal/), [EarnPark](https://earnpark.com/en/posts/bitcoin-fear-and-greed-index-explained-what-the-btc-sentiment-score-means-and-how-to-use-it/)).
- Buy-fear works "best with a long holding period"; sell-greed misses rallies; no F&G timing strategy beat buy-and-hold (548.8%) in one backtest ([Code Meets Capital](https://codemeetscapital.substack.com/p/backtesting-fear-and-greed-index), [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/how-a-bitcoin-fear-and-greed-index-trading-strategy-beats-buy-and-hold-investing)).
- Academic: sentiment/attention predicts crypto returns at **1–6 week** horizons (1-σ attention shock → +2.3% 2-week BTC; momentum effects 1–4 weeks) ([NBER w24877](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf), [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040162522005200)).
- Zero published evidence of an F&G/sentiment edge at sub-hour horizons; the index updates ~daily — it *cannot* carry 30-min information.

## 4. Microstructure math

Costs per round trip: fees 9bp RT + measured slip 6.8bp (if per-side: 13.6bp) → **15.8–22.6bp RT**. Cross-check: fee sweep implied real drag = 17.89−6.14 = **11.75bp/trip** — consistent.
From the actual tape (68 matched crypto trips, fills_live.tsv): median hold **1.43h** (p25 0.38h, p75 6.0h); median |gross move| 22.2bp; realized diffusion median **18.9bp/√hr**. Only 54% of trips even *moved* more than the 15.8bp cost.

Required gross edge (=cost) as a fraction of the typical move σ(h)=18.9√h bp:

| Hold | σ(h) | cost 15.8bp | cost 22.6bp |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30min | 13.4bp | **1.18σ** | 1.69σ |
| 4h | 37.8bp | 0.42σ | 0.60σ |
| 1d | 92.6bp | 0.17σ | 0.24σ |
| 3d | 160bp | **0.10σ** | 0.14σ |

A realistic signal captures ~0.05–0.1σ of the horizon move. So breakeven is *approachable only at ≥1–3 day holds*; at 30min the desk must predict >1 full standard deviation — categorically infeasible. Slip alone (6.8bp) is 51% of the 30-min σ; with 60s mark polling a 30-min hold gets ~30 mark observations and the 0.30% target cap (30bp) sits at 2.2σ of a 30-min move but only 0.19σ of a 3-day move.

## 5. Conclusion

Psychology's documented edge lives at **multi-day-to-quarter horizons, contrarian at extremes** — exactly where our cost math first becomes feasible (≥3d: cost ≈0.10σ). Our desk cycles at 30min with median hold 1.4h, where NO signal — psych or technical — can clear 15.8bp (the −6.14bp gross-at-zero-cost fee-sweep result already proved nothing clears even zero cost). Weighting psychology "higher" inside the current horizon adds prose to a structurally cost-doomed loop. The best-shot design: psych-gated **multi-day** positions — enter only at F&G extremes (≤20 contrarian long / ≥80 flat-or-short), hold 3+ days, sized by the regime engine that already exists but is disabled. In-window F&G 27–74 means such a strategy trades rarely — being flat this month would have beaten −169.06.
