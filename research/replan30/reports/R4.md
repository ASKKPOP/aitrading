# R4 — External research: profitable AI/algo crypto trading (GitHub + literature)

## 1. What profitable configs of open-source bots actually use

- **Freqtrade** treats the stoploss as the risk unit and builds profit capture around **trailing / ratcheting stops that only move up**, with ROI targets larger than or trailing beyond the stop — never the reverse ([stoploss docs](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/stoploss/), [custom_stoploss callbacks](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/strategy-callbacks/), [ATR-based R:R issue #9895](https://github.com/freqtrade/freqtrade/issues/9895)). Its most-used community strategy, [NostalgiaForInfinity](https://github.com/iterativv/NostalgiaForInfinity), is a high-trade-count multi-condition entry system whose maintainers explicitly warn about the backtest→live gap and overfitting; it is not evidence of a durable edge, and its live results are contested.
- **Hummingbot** (market-making) is candid: the spread must survive "fees, adverse selection, inventory drift, and execution risk", rebate/fee reduction is often the whole margin, and it's "not a passive side project" ([hummingbot.org/faq](https://hummingbot.org/faq/)). Market-making **requires order-book quoting far faster than 60s polling — NOT feasible for us** (constraint flagged).
- **FinRL / DRL**: spectacular backtests (360% annual, Sharpe 2.99) collapse to **0.66–0.74% cumulative, Sharpe 0.20–0.28 in live contest trading**; downside-penalizing rewards and explicit transaction-cost modeling are what stabilize real results ([FinRL Contests, arXiv:2504.02281](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.02281), [ensemble DRL crypto, arXiv:2309.00626](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.00626)).
- Consensus strategy classes that actually carry documented profit: **trend-following/momentum with wide payoff** (regime-gated), **delta-neutral funding carry**, and **professional market-making** (out of reach). Mean-reversion scalping is viable only with fees near zero and stops as tight as targets.

## 2. LLM-as-trader: documented edge?

- **Live, real money (the only test matching our architecture): Alpha Arena / Nof1** gave 6 frontier LLMs $10k each trading Hyperliquid perps autonomously (Oct–Nov 2025). **4 of 6 lost money** (GPT −63%, Gemini −57%, Grok −45%, Claude Sonnet −31%); only DeepSeek V3.1 (+~5%) and Qwen3-Max (+~23%) finished green. Nof1's own diagnosis: *"PnL was dominated by trading costs in early runs as agents over-traded and took quick, tiny gains that fees erased"* — literally our defect ([nof1.ai](https://nof1.ai/), [Protos: "LLMs can't trade crypto"](https://protos.com/llm-crypto-trading-contest-finds-llms-cant-trade-crypto/), [season-1 results](https://www.iweaver.ai/blog/alpha-arena-ai-trading-season-1-results/)). Winners were distinguished by **risk sizing and hold discipline, not prediction**.
- **Benchmarks**: [StockBench (arXiv:2510.02209)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02209) finds most LLM agents only modestly beat a weak 0.4% buy-hold window, and "strong performance on financial QA does not translate into effective trading". [TradingAgents (arXiv:2412.20138)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20138) and [FinMem (arXiv:2311.13743)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13743) report improved Sharpe/returns but **backtest-only, equities, no live verification**.
- Net: there is **no published evidence of an edge from an LLM making per-cycle hold/buy micro-decisions**. The documented LLM value is at the slower layer (position sizing, risk framing, regime/news synthesis, daily-cadence decisions).

## 3. Practitioner consensus on our exact failure (inverted R:R)

The high-win-rate/tiny-target/wide-stop scalper trap is a named, well-documented negative-expectancy pattern: "strategies that scalp small profits and occasionally take big losses show 80–90% win rates for months before one bad day erases everything"; widening stops raises win rate while collapsing expectancy ([CrossTrade: win rate vs expectancy](https://crosstrade.io/learn/performance-metrics/win-rate-vs-expectancy), [Fenwick, "The Win-Rate Trap"](https://medium.com/@JorrelFenwick/the-win-rate-trap-why-payoff-and-expectancy-drive-real-edge-3635bf13729a), [Tradezella expectancy](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/trading-expectancy)). Profitable geometries are bimodal:
- **Trend-following**: 10–40% win rate, payoff ≫1, small initial risk, wide/uncapped targets via trailing exits ([QuantifiedStrategies](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/mean-reversion-vs-trend-following/)).
- **True scalping**: ~1:1 R:R with stops AS TIGHT AS targets, needing >55–60% wins just to clear costs ([Zaye Capital](https://zayecapitalmarkets.com/good-win-rate-in-forex/)).
- Our shipped geometry (0.30% cap target, 72% of stops >1.5%, 28:1 stop:target) matches **neither**; the literature says shrink the stop or unleash the target — the target sweep (FACTS: no level's mean clears zero) already showed shrinking the target alone cannot fix it.

## 4. Crypto edges compatible with 60s mark polling

- **Time-series momentum**: Liu–Tsyvinski ([RFS 2021](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/34/6/2689/5912024), [NBER w24877](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf)) — daily/weekly BTC returns predict returns up to 8 weeks out; 1σ daily return → +0.33% next day. Daily-bar cadence; 60s polling trivially sufficient. Cross-sectional momentum ~3%/wk but concentrated in **large-cap** coins.
- **Donchian/ATR breakout with trailing exit + vol sizing**: crypto-adapted trend-following with Donchian ensembles and volatility sizing shows strong risk-adjusted returns ([Rohrbach et al.](https://assets.super.so/e46b77e7-ee08-445e-b43f-4ffd88ae0a0e/files/9c27aa78-9b14-4419-a53d-bc56fa9d43b2.pdf), [BTC trend-following 2021–2025 study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/395400190)). Bar-based; 60s-compatible.
- **Regime filters**: ADX<20 = don't trend-trade; ADX 25–40 + price>200MA = trend regime; ATR-ratio gates ([QuantMonitor regime filter](https://quantmonitor.net/how-to-identify-market-regimes-and-filter-strategies-by-trend-and-volatility/), [PyQuantLab regime-filtered trend](https://pyquantlab.medium.com/regime-filtered-trend-strategy-a-market-adaptive-trend-following-system-fa933e001237)). Cheap, 60s-compatible, and matches the "always hold" symptom: holding in a wrong regime is correct — the fix is trading the right class per regime, not forcing entries.
- **Fear&Greed**: works as a **filter on long entries** (fear-gated longs raise net profit — [QuantConnect research](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/19465/filtering-trades-with-the-fear-and-greed-index/)), not as a standalone contrarian signal; extreme-band strategies need extremes, and FACTS records our window only saw 27–74 — consistent with the prior finding that psych-conditional targets were unsupported.
- **Funding-rate capture**: delta-neutral spot-long/perp-short earns 10–30% APY in 2025–26 with <2% drawdowns, but only ~40% of top spreads survive costs and it **requires two simultaneous legs** ([Kraken guide](https://www.kraken.com/learn/futures-trading-funding-rate-arbitrage), [ScienceDirect risk/return study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2096720925000818)). Funding accrues hourly/8-hourly → 60s polling fine; **flag: needs the sim ledger to hold a spot leg + perp leg concurrently, and hedge-drift monitoring — an engine capability question, not a data-speed one**.

## KEY FINDINGS

1. **Our defect is a named, documented trap.** High win-rate/small-target/wide-stop = negative expectancy that "masks itself until one bad day"; widening stops raises win rate while collapsing expectancy — exactly our 46% win/0.22 payoff/28:1 stop:target. Evidence: [CrossTrade](https://crosstrade.io/learn/performance-metrics/win-rate-vs-expectancy), [Win-Rate Trap](https://medium.com/@JorrelFenwick/the-win-rate-trap-why-payoff-and-expectancy-drive-real-edge-3635bf13729a); FACTS.md lines 39–44.
2. **No published edge exists for per-cycle LLM hold/buy desks; the only live real-money test reproduced our failure mode.** Alpha Arena (Hyperliquid, real $): 4/6 frontier LLMs lost 31–63%; organizer's diagnosis = "over-traded, quick tiny gains that fees erased". Evidence: [nof1.ai](https://nof1.ai/), [Protos](https://protos.com/llm-crypto-trading-contest-finds-llms-cant-trade-crypto/). LLM value shown only in risk-sizing/slow-cadence layers ([StockBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02209)).
3. **Profitable geometry is bimodal and ours is neither pole.** Trend-following: 10–40% wins, payoff ≫1, trailing exits. Scalping: ~1:1 R:R, stops as tight as targets, near-zero fees. Literature says flip stop:target below 1:1 targeted risk or trail uncapped targets — our target sweep (no target level clears zero, FACTS line 43) independently confirms shrinking targets can't fix it.
4. **Strongest 60s-compatible documented crypto edge is daily/weekly time-series momentum.** 1σ daily BTC return → +0.33% next-day; predictability to 8 weeks; strongest in large caps. Evidence: [Liu–Tsyvinski, RFS 2021](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/34/6/2689/5912024).
5. **Regime gating (ADX/200MA/ATR-ratio) is the standard fix for "wrong strategy, wrong regime"** — trend-trade only in trend regimes, stand down or mean-revert in chop. Evidence: [QuantMonitor](https://quantmonitor.net/how-to-identify-market-regimes-and-filter-strategies-by-trend-and-volatility/), [QuantifiedStrategies](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/mean-reversion-vs-trend-following/). "Always hold" in chop is correct behavior; profit requires an edge class to switch INTO, not looser gates (consistent with owner constraint).
6. **Three implementable patterns with links:** (a) **Donchian/ATR breakout + chandelier trailing exit + vol-parity sizing** — [Rohrbach et al. paper](https://assets.super.so/e46b77e7-ee08-445e-b43f-4ffd88ae0a0e/files/9c27aa78-9b14-4419-a53d-bc56fa9d43b2.pdf), mechanics in [freqtrade custom_stoploss](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/strategy-callbacks/); (b) **daily time-series momentum overlay** (long-only exposure switch on trailing 1d/7d sign) — [NBER w24877](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf); (c) **funding-rate capture** — [Kraken guide](https://www.kraken.com/learn/futures-trading-funding-rate-arbitrage) + [risk/return study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2096720925000818) — **flagged: needs two-leg (spot+perp) support in the ledger; only ~40% of spreads survive costs**; all three run on bar data ≥60s, none needs order-book speed.
7. **Market-making/maker-rebate strategies are excluded by our 60s constraint.** Hummingbot's own docs: the edge is quoting speed + fee rebates; adverse selection eats slow quoters. Evidence: [hummingbot FAQ](https://hummingbot.org/faq/).
8. **DRL/backtest claims deflate ~500x live.** FinRL backtest 360%/yr → live contest 0.66–0.74% cumulative ([FinRL Contests](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.02281)). Any Plan-30 candidate must be judged on live-window sim P&L, not backtest, matching the owner's experimental-mindset rule.
