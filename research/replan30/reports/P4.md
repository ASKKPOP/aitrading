# P4 — Radical Plan: Retire the 30-Minute Scalp Loop, Trade the Trend Pole

**Premise (binding, from FACTS + R5):** the current class — LLM micro-decisions every 30 min, median hold 1.43h — pays 15.8–22.6bp RT cost against a 13.4bp 30-min move: the desk must predict >1σ to break even, which no signal does (−6.14bp gross at ZERO fee, CI90<0). R4: the only live real-money LLM test (Alpha Arena) reproduced this exact failure; documented profitable geometry is bimodal and ours is neither pole. Tuning cannot fix a class that is cost-infeasible at its own horizon. This plan changes the class. Everything runs on the sim ledger, 60s marks, existing engine; judged on 30-day live-window sim P&L (not backtest — R4 F8: DRL claims deflate ~500x).

The LLM desk gets one daily decision: veto/size the signal (Alpha Arena winners were distinguished by sizing and hold discipline, not prediction — R4 F2).
(b) P&L: daily time-series momentum is the strongest 60s-compatible documented edge (1σ daily return → +0.33% next day, predictability to 8 weeks, large caps — R4 F4). At 3-day holds σ≈160bp and cost 15.8bp = 0.10σ; capturing a realistic 0.05–0.1σ ≈ **+8 to +16bp/trip net** vs the current −17.89bp. Geometry moves to the trend pole (10–40% WR, payoff ≫1 — R4 F3), matching the tape's own evidence that long holds with wide exits are the only positive bucket.
(c) Falsifier: pre-registered 30-day sim, ≥20 trips: mean/trip ≤0, or worse than staying flat, or payoff <1.5 → kill the module. No live-account promotion before the sim result.
