11. What this plan is not

— Not a promise US equities out-earn crypto: the book says the opposite today, on a tiny sample.
— Not a fast-trading strategy: the 1–2 day floor is load-bearing.
— Not "our AI is smarter": the edge is checkable differentiators and a measured hit-rate, or it is nothing.
— Not one strategy: v1-PEAD and v2-fade are two edges with two gates — passing one says nothing about the other.
