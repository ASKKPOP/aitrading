1. Purpose — what this exists to do

목적 — 데스크가 이미 가진 원칙을, 그것이 적용되는 바로 그 상황에서, 검증된 것만 읽게 만든다. 새 알파 발굴이 아니라 측정된 손실 형태를 막는 것.

The desk already has the doctrine: 36 skills, written from measured episodes. The problem is delivery — everything is sent to every decision, so the situation-specific lesson competes with 48,000 chars of doctrine that does not apply. The learning daemon's purpose is to close a loop the platform cannot close by hand:

questionanswer
What does it learn?Which lesson, shown in which situation, changes the desk's behavior in the direction the loss-shape audits already priced (dump→reduce, round-trip→bank, no adds into losers, fee-hurdle discipline, arm usage).
What does it NOT learn?Alpha. Ranking 2 recall chunks better does not find new trades. It also never touches gates, sizing, risk rails or the mandate — those stay code.
Why is this the profit path?74% of all measured loss came from ONE behavior class (full-book dumps on sub-2% moves, 6 of 84 closes). Making the desk reliably apply existing doctrine in the moment it applies attacks the largest measured leak — plus a deterministic LLM-cost win (§6).

2. The prompt today — where variance exists and where it does not

현재 프롬프트 구성 — 학습 루프가 변화시킬 수 있는 건 전체 원칙 텍스트의 약 3%(리콜 라인)뿐. 48,000자 스킬 월은 구조적으로 분산 0.

Finding 1 of the deep analysis: the loop under construction learns over the smallest channel. Finding 2: "Phase 1" shipped as the desk_memory corpus — the designed atomisation of the skills wall was never done, so 97% of what the desk reads still never varies.

4. Why the reward is behavior, not P&L — the identifiability math

보상이 P&L이 아니라 행동인 이유 — 청크별 원시 P&L 귀속은 현재 물량에서 통계적으로 식별 불가(수년). 행동 대리지표는 수주면 판정 가능. P&L은 승격 게이트에서 포트폴리오 단위로만.

6. Target — where "plus profit" actually comes from

목표 — 플러스 수익의 실제 출처. 확정적 이득(비용)부터, 행동 개선, 그리고 정직하게: 알파는 약속하지 않는다.

sourcenaturemechanism
LLM prompt costdeterministicThe skills wall is ~12k tokens per decision prompt, every cycle, every run, forever. Situational top-K (Phase 4 / step e) cuts it 60–80% — a guaranteed, compounding spend reduction. NET P&L by definition.
Capacity headroomdeterministicThe block cap was raised 4× and once had 6 chars of headroom; #287 showed the cap silently costing a lesson. Retrieval ends the recurring tax.
Loss-shape reductionmeasured behavioral74% of measured loss sat in one behavior class. Lessons that demonstrably shift that behavior (link 1) attack the book's largest leak — the daemon's real job.
Retrieval alphanot claimedRanking 2 recall chunks better is unlikely to create new alpha. If the promotion gate ever measures positive P&L beyond cost + loss-shape, that is upside, not the plan.
