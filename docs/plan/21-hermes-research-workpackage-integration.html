6. Prompt-budget check for the two Phase-1 skills

프롬프트 예산 검증: 두 스킬 추가 후에도 캡 이내지만, 커스텀 스킬 여유가 1k 미만으로 줄어 캡 상향(20k→24k)을 함께 반영

Numbers are estimates from the catalogue comment (17,859 at defaults) plus ~1.1–1.2k per new body; the test recomputes the real totals and is the arbiter. A skill that would overflow is skipped whole, never truncated — which is exactly why headroom, not fit, is the invariant.
