The one-sentence diagnosis: the desk asks its LLM to verbalize a confidence number — a technique that research shows is systematically overconfident and barely quantized — while the inputs that actually predict outcomes (thesis_alignment, momentum_quality) get averaged away inside that one broken scalar, and nothing ever feeds trade outcomes back into the number.

한 문장 진단: 데스크는 LLM에게 신뢰도 숫자를 "말로 표현"하게 시키는데(연구상 체계적으로 과신·양자화되는 방식), 정작 결과를 예측하는 입력(thesis_alignment, momentum_quality)은 그 고장난 스칼라 안에서 평균되어 사라지고, 거래 결과가 그 숫자로 피드백되는 일은 전혀 없다.

1 · The problem, measured on our own book

문제를 우리 장부에서 직접 측정

1a. Confidence is not calibrated — claimed vs delivered

The desk claims 55–75% and delivers 0–33% — and its most-confident bucket (≥0.70) wins the least (14%). A confidence number this far from reality cannot gate trades. 데스크는 55–75%를 주장하고 0–33%를 낸다 — 가장 확신한 구간(≥0.70)의 승률이 최저(14%). 이런 숫자는 게이트가 될 수 없다.

1b. The scale is barely used — 3 numbers = 63% of all decisions

The model has collapsed onto a few habitual numbers (0.58, 0.62, 0.55) hovering just under/over the 0.55–0.65 gates it knows about — classic anchor behavior, not probability estimation. 모델은 게이트 문턱 주변의 습관적 숫자 몇 개(0.58, 0.62, 0.55)로 붕괴했다 — 확률 추정이 아니라 앵커 행동.

1c. The signal exists — it's in the sub-scores, and the scalar buries it

Two of the four inputs genuinely separate winners from losers (thesis +0.073, momentum +0.081), one is anti-signal (entry_quality −0.074), one is noise. Averaging them into one verbalized scalar destroys exactly the information a gate needs. 4개 입력 중 2개(thesis, momentum)는 실제로 승패를 구분하고, 1개(entry_quality)는 역신호, 1개는 노이즈다. 이를 하나의 스칼라로 뭉개는 순간 게이트에 필요한 정보가 파괴된다.

2 · Why this happens (research-grounded)

왜 이런 일이 생기는가 (연구 근거)

  • Verbalized confidence is a known-broken instrument. LLMs asked to state a confidence number are systematically overconfident, and the failure is structural — recent work traces it to identifiable internal circuits, and benchmarks (ConfidenceBench) document that verbalized scores "fail to distinguish reliable answers from unreliable ones." Smaller models are worse; ours is a 120B local model asked to compress a market read into one number.
  • Quantization is expected behavior, not a bug of our prompt alone. Models collapse onto habitual "safe" values — ours learned 0.58/0.62 because those sit exactly around the platform's historical 0.55/0.65 gates. The number is an anchor negotiation with the gate, not a probability.
  • No feedback loop = no calibration, ever. Calibration is a mapping from claimed probability to observed frequency. Today nothing maps trade outcomes back onto the score. Without that loop even a perfect model drifts; with it, even a mediocre score becomes honest (this is standard Platt/isotonic recalibration, and with our n≈100, Platt scaling is the right small-sample tool).
  • Agreement beats self-report. Research consistently finds that self-consistency — sample the decision K times and use the agreement rate — is a more reliable confidence signal than any verbalized number; unanimous agreement is "nearly always correct" in graded tasks, and cross-model agreement adds an independent check.
  • Important honesty: calibration does not manufacture alpha. It makes the gate honest so expectancy math (p·win − (1−p)·loss − fees) can refuse bad trades and size good ones. The alpha itself lives where the data says it lives: thesis/momentum quality, R:R ~2:1, and the trailing lock that banks big winners.

4 · Step-by-step rollout (each ships dark, each is measured)

단계별 적용 — 전부 다크 출하, 전부 Brier 점수로 측정, 이기는 것만 승격

Step 0 · Score it — the foundation Nightly calibration scoreboard. A worker job computes the Brier score and the calibration curve (like §1a) from the already-journaled decisions+outcomes. Zero trading risk — it only reads. From this day on, every idea below is judged by one number: did Brier go down? 매일 밤 Brier 점수 + 캘리브레이션 곡선을 자동 산출. 거래 위험 0. 이후 모든 아이디어는 "Brier가 내려갔는가" 하나로 판정.
Step 1 · Recalibrate the existing number (cheap, ~1 week of data needed) Platt scaling on our own outcomes. Fit p(win) = σ(a·conf + b) on closed runs; refit weekly. "0.62" stops meaning 62% and starts meaning what it has actually delivered (~21%). Gate and sizing consume the calibrated p. Platt (not isotonic) because n≈100 — isotonic overfits small samples. Flag: RUN_CONF_CALIBRATED=shadow→apply. 우리 결과로 Platt 보정 맵을 학습, 주간 재적합. 게이트는 보정된 p를 사용. 표본이 작으므로 isotonic이 아닌 Platt.
Step 2 · Stop burying the signal — score from sub-scores A tiny meta-model instead of the verbalized scalar. Logistic regression on {thesis_alignment, momentum_quality, entry_quality (learns its own negative weight), risk_context, regime, fee-adjusted expected move} → p(win). Runs in shadow next to the scalar; promoted only when its Brier beats Step 1's for 2+ consecutive weeks. This is where 확신도 becomes a computed quantity, not a vibe. Flag: RUN_META_SCORE. 말로 만든 스칼라 대신 하위 점수들로 학습한 소형 로지스틱 모델이 p(win)을 계산. 그림자 모드로 나란히 실행, Brier가 2주 연속 이길 때만 승격.
Step 3 · Self-consistency — confidence from agreement, not self-report Sample the decision K=3 times on borderline reads only (the decision-routing pattern already used for E1). Agreement rate is the structural confidence: 3/3 buy = strong; 2/3 = weak; split = no trade. The local model makes the marginal cost compute-time, not dollars. Add one cross-model vote (claude-sonnet-5, already wired in per-tier routing) on large-notional entries only. Flags: RUN_DECISION_ENSEMBLE_K, RUN_CROSS_MODEL_VOTE. 경계 구간 결정만 K=3회 샘플링 — 일치율이 구조적 확신도. 대형 주문에는 sonnet-5 교차 투표 1회. 로컬 모델이라 추가 비용은 시간뿐.
Step 4 · Fix the elicitation — stop asking for a number it can't give Evidence-count elicitation + anchor removal. The prompt stops asking "confidence 0–1" and instead asks for countable evidence: N checkable factors for, M against, each named; the score derives from the tally. Never reveal gate thresholds in the prompt (0.58/0.62 are learned anchors). Introspective CoT before scoring — research shows both reduce verbalized overconfidence. A/B against Step 2 by Brier. "0~1 신뢰도"를 묻지 말고 셀 수 있는 근거(찬성 N개/반대 M개)를 요구, 점수는 집계에서 도출. 프롬프트에 게이트 문턱 노출 금지(앵커 제거).
Step 5 · Keep it honest forever — the loop, conditioned Weekly auto-refit + regime/market conditioning + cohort A/B. As data grows: per-market calibration maps (crypto ≠ forex), regime features, and a permanent A/B (calibrated-gate fleet vs control fleet) so every future model swap (e.g. 20b→120b→Sonnet) is judged by Brier + realized expectancy, not by anyone's impression. 주간 자동 재적합 + 시장/레짐별 보정 + 상시 A/B. 이후 모델 교체는 인상이 아니라 Brier + 실현 기대값으로 판정.

5 · What success looks like (and what it won't do)

성공의 모습 — 그리고 이것이 해주지 않는 것

  • Success metric #1 — Brier score falls. Today's implied Brier on entries is ≈0.30+ (claiming ~0.62 on a 21% base). A calibrated map alone should land near 0.17 (the base-rate Brier); the meta-model must beat that to earn its place.
  • Success metric #2 — the calibration curve becomes monotonic. Higher score → genuinely higher win rate. The ≥0.70-wins-14% inversion disappears by construction once outcomes feed the map.
  • Success metric #3 — expectancy-gated entries. Trades fire only when calibrated p clears the fee-adjusted expectancy line; sizing scales with p (Kelly-lite, capped). Fewer, better trades — the 69%-hold discipline is already there.
  • What it will NOT do: invent alpha. If thesis/momentum stop predicting, no calibration saves it — but then the scoreboard will say so within a week, which is precisely the point: every future claim about the desk's judgment becomes checkable.
