02Incident 1 — fleet 43 pinned at 1/8 closedcaused by my own ship

Owner-reported ("why doesn't increase to 8?"). Monitoring evidence traced a three-stage mechanism — and the first stage was a rail I had shipped that same morning.

Symptom
Swing fleet 43 (8 slots, us-stock) stuck at replenishing:1/8 all day.
Cause chain
① The new no_prospect_switched rail (owner's 1–5h switch order) treated a swing slot's designed flat waiting as "no profit future" — 8 swing builds killed in one session, each rebuild dying exactly at its 3-hour mark.
② Every kill benched its symbol for 12 hours, draining the tech-only candidate pool.
③ The backtest expectancy gate then rightly refused the remainder on real losing history (TSLA −$494/trade over 6 closed, AAPL −$426/8, PLTR −$576/5) → zero buildable candidates.
Fix
Swing archetype exempted from the rail (cf94b2a2, image 20260831-swing-exempt); the intraday archetype keeps the owner's 1–5h switch unchanged. Stages ② and ③ were left alone — both protect money and worked as designed.
Proof of recovery
QCOM survived past the 3-hour mark that killed all its predecessors; slots rebuilt exactly as each bench lapsed: 1 → 3 → 5 → 6 of 8 overnight, every step matching the predicted bench-expiry time.

03Incident 2 — macro calendar rate-limited (HTTP 429) closed

The only feed outage of the 48 hours, caught by the 2-consecutive-cycles rule at 23:26 UTC and root-caused within the same cycle.

Symptom
macro_calendar (faireconomy / ForexFactory) answered 429 Too Many Requests, retry-after 157 for 2 consecutive cycles.
Root cause
The new crawl pipeline (live since 08:57Z that morning) fetched the weekly calendar once per symbol per hourly pass — 112 requests in one day for a dataset that changes weekly. The host rate-limited the box's IP.
Fix
The four symbol-independent feeds (calendar, macro regime, fear-greed, benchmark candles) are now fetched once per pass and handed to every symbol's evaluation (21fb51c3, image 20260831-calendar-429) — an ~8× traffic cut. A regression test counts fetches across a 3-symbol pass; a deliberately injected bug in the fix was caught by it before shipping.
Proof of recovery
The feed answered OK one cycle after deploy and has passed every one of the 24 cycles since.

  • The rule being applied: Monday is excluded from the count (my broken rail contaminated it), so Tuesday is clean zero-entry session #1. If Wednesday also closes entry-less, this becomes a formal finding with a counterfactual: replay what forced entries at the desk's near-miss moments would have earned or lost versus the WAITs.
