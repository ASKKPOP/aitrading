① The Top 10 Parameters, Ranked

Color shows the data family each parameter belongs to. Hover any row for what it measures and how AI models use it.

Price & market data Market microstructure Text & sentiment Macro & fundamentals
1
Price momentum / trend12-month return (skip last month), industry momentum
Measures: whether price has been persistently rising or falling (trend continuation).
Why #1: the most informative predictor family across every ML model class in the benchmark study (Gu–Kelly–Xiu, RFS 2020).
How AI uses it: core return-forecast feature; simple rules use it directly ("price above SMA → buy").
2
Technical indicatorsRSI, MACD, SMA/EMA, Stochastic, Williams %R
Measures: overbought/oversold conditions and trend strength via engineered price/volume transforms.
Why #2: the most common ML input features across 138 surveyed studies (2,173 catalogued variables); ~66% of ML stock-prediction studies use them.
How AI uses it: engineered features fed to classifiers/regressors.
3
Volume & liquidityDollar volume, market cap, bid–ask spread
Measures: participation/conviction behind moves and how cheaply a position can be traded.
Why #3: second tier of ML feature importance; ablation tests show performance degrades when volume features are removed.
How AI uses it: predictor + tradability filter for position sizing.
4
Volatility & riskReturn volatility, idiosyncratic vol, beta
Measures: dispersion of returns and systematic-risk exposure.
Why #4: third tier of leading predictors in all ML model classes.
How AI uses it: return predictor + position sizing / risk-adjusted signal weighting.
5
Raw OHLCV price seriesOpen, High, Low, Close, Volume
Measures: the unprocessed market record.
Why #5: the baseline deep-learning input; at minute-level frequency raw price features outranked 13 technical indicators.
How AI uses it: LSTM/CNN models ingest the raw series and learn representations end-to-end.
6
Limit order book depth10 levels of bids/asks = 40-feature input
Measures: real-time supply and demand — prices & volumes waiting at each depth level.
Why #6: orders behind the best bid/ask carry ~22% of price discovery; the dominant input at intraday/HFT horizons.
How AI uses it: standard input for short-horizon deep models (DeepLOB) and execution algorithms.
7
News & social sentimentNews wires, X/Twitter, StockTwits, Reddit via NLP
Measures: aggregate market mood extracted by NLP models.
Why #7: standard industry input; overnight sentiment predicts next-day S&P 500 returns (~6bp per SD, Fed research).
How AI uses it: converted to numeric sentiment scores per asset/day and merged with price features.
8
Macroeconomic dataRates, 10Y yield, PMI, GDP, uncertainty indices
Measures: the discount-rate and growth environment.
Why #8: one of the three canonical feature families; most useful for index-level and longer-horizon forecasts.
How AI uses it: slow-moving conditioning features that shift the model's regime (risk-on/risk-off).
9
Fundamental dataROE, EPS, valuation ratios
Measures: firm quality and value from financial statements.
Why #9: canonical family but used in ~23% of studies vs ~66% for technicals; value is a return predictor "everywhere".
How AI uses it: cross-sectional ranking at weekly–monthly horizons — buy cheap/quality, short expensive/weak.
10
Mean-reversion signalsDeviation from average, pairs spreads, 1-month reversal
Measures: how far price (or a spread between related assets) has stretched from its historical average.
Why #10: the canonical counterpart to momentum; short-term reversal also ranks among top ML features.
How AI uses it: pairs trading / stat-arb — buy the underpriced leg, sell the overpriced one.

Bar length reflects rank order (a composite of measured ML feature importance for ranks 1–4 and prevalence of use in the literature for ranks 5–10) — it is not a measured quantity. Sources: Gu–Kelly–Xiu (RFS 2020); Kumbure et al. (2022); Sezer et al. (2020).

STAGE 1

Feature Engineering

Compute indicator values, momentum windows (e.g., 12-month skip-month), normalize across stocks, score text into sentiment numbers, lag features to avoid look-ahead bias.

▼
STAGE 2

AI Model — Return Forecast

Gradient-boosted trees / neural networks (LSTM, CNN) predict expected return or up/down probability per asset. Nonlinear models roughly double the out-of-sample Sharpe ratio vs linear regression (1.35 vs 0.61) on the same inputs.

▼
STAGE 3

Signal Generation

Forecasts are ranked or thresholded: strong positive forecast → buy candidate; strong negative → sell/short candidate; weak signal → no trade.

▼
STAGE 4

Risk & Position Sizing

Volatility (#4) sets position size; liquidity (#3) caps order size; portfolio constraints and drawdown limits apply. A great signal in an untradable stock is discarded here.

▼send order

BUY

Execution algo works the order using order-book depth (#6) to minimize impact

HOLD / NO TRADE

Signal below threshold, or trading cost exceeds expected edge

SELL / SHORT

Exit on reversal signal, stop-loss, target hit, or negative forecast

↻ Outcomes feed back into the model — retraining and walk-forward validation close the loop.

Bottom line: chart patterns are statistically informative but not proven profitable stand-alone; human-psychology signals add real but modest information (expect 50–55% directional accuracy, not 87.6%). The measured edge comes from combining many weak signals in a nonlinear model — not from any single parameter. Distrust any accuracy claim above ~60% that lacks out-of-sample, cost-adjusted, replicated evidence.

⑤ Final Hybrid Strategy: Deploying the Top 10 Parameters

The conclusion of all research rounds, unified into one strategy. Psychology sets the regime (WHEN), charts set the trigger (WHAT/at what price), volume + sentiment confirm, and volatility sets the size. This maps each Top-10 parameter to its place in the 4-layer stack. Trade only on confluence — 4 or more of 6 conditions agreeing.

#2 Technical indicators / patternsLayer 3 — trigger. Pattern completion times the entry. Never standalone — 53–54% win rate at best; always requires volume confirmationBuy B3 / Sell S3

Execution summary: ① VIX & events set the mode (trend / contrarian / stand-aside) → ② momentum + MA set direction → ③ pattern + volume + sentiment confirm the trigger → ④ volatility sets size; the stop-loss is absolute. Act only at 4+ of 6 conditions. Realistic target accuracy is 55–58% — the edge comes from stacking signals, skipping event windows, and cutting losers early, not from prediction.
