# The Hybrid Framework: Mixing Chart Patterns × Human Psychology
## 차트 패턴 × 인간 심리 융합 매매 프레임워크

**Built from two rounds of verified deep research (200+ agents, primary-source fact-checked) · July 2026**
**⚠️ Educational framework, not investment advice. Realistic expectation: 50–55% directional accuracy — the edge comes from confluence + risk management, not any single signal.**

---

## 1. The core principle the research proved

Two verified facts drive the whole design:

1. **Chart patterns are informative but not profitable alone** (Lo–Mamaysky–Wang 2000; Savin 2007). They tell you *something* is happening, with a 53–54% edge at best — never use them as a standalone trigger.
2. **Psychology moves markets but is only tradable at extremes and at specific times** (overnight sentiment ~6bp/SD; sentiment accuracy 50–55%; panic/euphoria are where behavioral effects concentrate).

So the mix is: **psychology decides the REGIME (when, and how much risk); charts decide the TRIGGER (what and at what price); volume + sentiment CONFIRM; volatility decides the SIZE.**

Signals are additive, not absolute: each satisfied condition adds a point; you act only on confluence (4+ of 6), because a 53% signal stacked with independent 53% signals is how real systems get past 55%.

## 2. The four-layer decision stack

### LAYER 1 — Psychology regime filter (인간 심리 = 국면 판단) — *"Should I trade at all, and which mode?"*
| Input | State | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| VIX / fear level | < 20 calm | **Trend mode** — follow charts |
| | 20–30 nervous | Reduce size 50%, tighten stops |
| | > 30 panic | **Contrarian mode** — ignore bearish chart patterns, prepare mean-reversion buys |
| Event calendar (FOMC, CPI, elections) | within ±1 day | **No new entries**; hold or hedge |
| War / policy shock headline | first 1–3 days | Never buy the first bounce; wait for stabilization signal |
| Social/news sentiment tone | extreme euphoria | Contrarian sell-alert on |
| | extreme fear | Contrarian buy-alert on |

### LAYER 2 — Direction engine (charts: WHAT) — the verified strongest predictors
- 12-month momentum (skip last month) positive → long bias / negative → short bias
- Price vs 200-day MA (golden-cross state) → trend direction
- 1-month short-term reversal → counter-trend timing inside the bias

### LAYER 3 — Trigger & confirmation (charts + psychology together: WHEN)
A pattern only counts when **completed AND volume-confirmed AND sentiment doesn't contradict**:
- Bullish trigger: double-bottom / H&S-inverse breakout **+ rising volume + non-negative overnight sentiment**
- Bearish trigger: double-top / H&S completion **+ rising volume + deteriorating news tone**
- No volume = no trade (volume features are verified essential; Dinh & Kwon 2018)

### LAYER 4 — Size & exit (risk: HOW MUCH)
- Position size = risk budget ÷ volatility (verified: volatility is a top-4 predictor and the sizing input)
- Hard stop below pattern invalidation point; trail with MA
- Time stop: thesis not working in N days → exit (your accuracy is ~55%, so losers must be cut fast)

## 3. The BUY checklist (confluence ≥ 4 of 6 → buy)

| # | Signal | Source side | Verified basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Price above 200-day MA / golden-cross state | Chart | Brock 1992: buy-signal days +12% annualized |
| B2 | Positive 12-1 momentum | Chart | GKX 2020: #1 ML predictor |
| B3 | Bullish pattern completed WITH volume | Chart | LMW 2000 informative + volume essential |
| B4 | Overnight/news sentiment positive or recovering | Psychology | Fed: ~6bp/SD next-day |
| B5 | Fear spike fading (VIX falling from >30 peak) | Psychology | Overreaction/mean-reversion literature |
| B6 | No FOMC/CPI/major event within 24h | Psychology | Event-risk timing |

**Contrarian special (panic buy):** VIX > 30 + sentiment at extreme fear + price stabilizing above short MA + volume climax passed → mean-reversion buy with half size. (This is the "be greedy when others are fearful" case, systematized.)

## 4. The SELL checklist (confluence ≥ 4 of 6 → sell/avoid)

| # | Signal | Source side |

## 5. WHEN × WHAT scenario matrix

| Scenario (market psychology state) | Dominant side | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Calm uptrend, positive sentiment | Charts | Trend-follow: buy pullbacks to MA with volume; hold |
| Calm downtrend | Charts | Stay out or short bias; don't catch knives without Layer-1 panic signal |
| Panic (war headline, VIX >30, fear extreme) | Psychology | Day 1–3: do nothing. Then contrarian buy on stabilization trigger (B5) |
| Euphoria (parabolic price + everyone bullish) | Psychology | Take profits into strength; sell triggers S4; never add |
| FOMC/CPI/election window | Psychology | Flat/no new entries; resume next day |
| Pattern breakout without volume | Neither | Trap risk — skip (most false breakouts live here) |
| Chart bullish BUT sentiment collapsing on real news | Psychology vetoes | Skip the trade — news regime beats stale chart |
| Sentiment noisy, no extreme, chart clear | Charts | Trade the chart; sentiment is only a tiebreaker |

## 6. Expectation management (verified numbers)

- Directional accuracy to expect: **50–55%**, not 80%.
- Golden-cross type edge: ~4–5 win-rate points over a coin flip, before costs.
- Sentiment edge: basis points per day, real only at extremes and overnight.
- The compounding edge comes from: confluence (stacking independent weak edges), cutting losers fast, sizing by volatility, and skipping event windows — not from prediction genius.
- Every published edge decays. Backtest, paper-trade, and re-validate out-of-sample before risking money.
