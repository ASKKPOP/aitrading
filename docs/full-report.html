4Safety posture

  • Only the simulator account can ever execute. Real-account rules run analysis and record the decision, but the executor refuses them — verified with a forced 99%-confidence BUY.
  • No real-exchange keys are stored. The Accounts page collects setup but deliberately does not persist secrets or enable live orders. Going live is a separate, explicit future step (secure server-side key storage + your sign-off).
  • US market hours respected — us-stock rules skip execution while the market is closed; crypto runs 24/7.
  • Spend guards — buys require sufficient cash; sells cap at the existing position; every run is recorded with its reason.
