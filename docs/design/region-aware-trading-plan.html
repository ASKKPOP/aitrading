Region-aware trading

Sooppiy serves nine countries in six languages. Region decides when a member can trade, what they may hold, and which venue fills it. Today the platform models none of that — it models language, and quietly assumes New York.

The one mistake this plan exists to prevent

Language is not region. English covers the USA, Canada, Australia and Singapore — four countries, four time zones, four regulators, two hemispheres of daylight saving. Keying regional behaviour off users.lang would put a Sydney member on a New York clock and call it done. Region has to be its own field.

The product conclusion, before any code

For eight of the nine countries, crypto, forex and futures are the product — they are the only markets open when those members are awake. US equities are a nights-and-weekends feature outside North America. Region-awareness is therefore not only a compliance chore; it changes what the platform should lead with per region.

4 · The model

Three fields, deliberately separate, because they answer three different questions and change at different rates.

users.regionISO-3166 country: US CA AU SG KR JP TH VN ID. Answers what may I trade and under whose rules. Changes rarely; needs an audit trail.
users.timezoneIANA zone: Asia/Seoul. Answers what time is it for you. Derived from region by default, separately overridable — members travel.
users.langAlready shipped. Answers what words do you read. Deliberately independent: a Korean speaker in Singapore is lang=ko, region=SG.
Not a single "locale"The tempting shortcut. It collapses three independent axes into one and makes the Korean-speaker-in-Singapore case unrepresentable. Do not do this.

Derive the allowlist, never enumerate it

This codebase has been bitten three times by hand-written market lists that went stale the moment a market was unblocked — most recently on 2026-07-28, when enabling per-market fees let forex trade for the first time and froze the fleet twenty minutes later because REFRESH_PRICED_MARKETS had been written back when forex could never trade. A per-region allow list repeats that mistake by construction. Use tradeable_markets() − deny[region], and pin it with a test that fails when a new market appears in the registry.

6 · Exchanges and venues

Region affects routing in two different places, and only one of them exists yet.

Price source — shippedmarkets.REGISTRY.price_provider already picks per market: Hyperliquid for crypto, Yahoo for equities/FX/futures, Polymarket for prediction. This is market-keyed and needs no region dimension. Leave it alone.
Order routing — the gapexecution/ has Alpaca, Binance and IBKR-CPAPI adapters selected by the member's own connected gateway. Nothing maps region → permitted broker. A Korean member connecting Alpaca is neither prevented nor flagged.

7 · Phasing

P1
Persist what is already being computed. users.region, users.timezone; store the country signup_lang already resolves; settings UI to confirm and correct; render every timestamp in the member's zone instead of the browser's. No behaviour gating. Ships dark, changes nothing, and starts collecting the field the rest of the plan needs.
Small. Touches signup, one migration, the settings page, and time formatting.
P2
Region-local time everywhere it matters. Session countdowns ("US market opens in 4h 20m" in their clock), fleet schedules, notification send-time windows — a hot_issue alert at 03:00 local is a worse product than no alert. Still no entitlement gating.
Depends on P1. Mostly presentation, plus a quiet-hours rule in alerts.send_alert.
P3
Entitlement gating. region_policy.py with the deny sets, the region_blocked reason in all six languages, run-build and order-path enforcement, and the derived-not-enumerated test. Dark-shipped behind REGION_POLICY_ENABLED like every other money path here.
Needs the matrix in §5 answered first. Gating on a guess is worse than not gating.
P4
Regional venue routing. Region → permitted broker set, and the domestic crypto adapters (Upbit, bitFlyer, Bitkub). Largest phase by far and the only one that is genuinely new infrastructure rather than a field and a lookup.
Each adapter is a build in its own right. Sequence by member count, not by table order.

8 · What I would get wrong

  • Daylight saving is not decoration. The US, Canada and Australia observe it; Korea, Japan, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Indonesia do not — and Australia's runs opposite to the northern hemisphere. Every band in §2 shifts twice a year, and the Sydney↔New York gap changes by two hours. Store IANA zones and compute; never store a UTC offset.
  • The matrix in §5 is my starting position, not a legal one. Retail crypto and FX are constrained in several of these countries in ways I cannot rule on. P3 must not ship until someone qualified has answered each review cell.
  • IP geolocation is a guess, and VPNs exist. It is fine for a default and unfit as a compliance control. If regional entitlement ever becomes a regulatory boundary rather than a product one, it needs declared residence with evidence, not an IP lookup.
  • Existing members have no region. Backfilling from lang would place every English speaker in the US — precisely the mistake this plan opens by warning about. They should be null until they tell us, and P3's gating has to behave sensibly for a null region on day one.
  • A new reason code without a translation is a shipped bug. region_blocked needs all six languages in the same commit. This has already happened once: stop_event:price_cross was classified but untranslated and would have rendered as a raw identifier inside a Korean sentence.
Recommended first move

Phase 1 alone, this week. It is small, it is reversible, it gates nothing, and it starts capturing a field the platform currently computes and discards on every signup. Everything after it is a decision about markets and licensing — which is a business question, and should not be blocked on the schema.
