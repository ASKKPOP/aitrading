What the full AI desk reads LIVE

When the AI desk takes a decision, it doesn't just look at the chart. Four analysts each write a brief, then a trader and a risk manager debate the final call:

  • News analyst — market and macro headlines, with sentiment
  • Psychology analyst — the human side: crowd mood, fear & greed, social buzz
  • Macro & policy analyst — economic regime signals and policy changes
  • Technical analyst — price structure, moving averages, momentum, volatility

This means human factors — how people feel about the market — are part of every critical decision, not just the chart.

Automatic profit protection LIVE

Two protections now run on every new fleet, without you doing anything:

🛡️ A safety stop sized to your actual position. If a trade moves genuinely against you, it's cut early — small, controlled losses instead of large ones.

🔒 A trailing profit lock. When a position builds a meaningful gain, the platform tracks its best level. If the gain starts slipping away past a set portion of that peak, the profit is banked automatically — so a winner doesn't quietly round-trip back to zero while you sleep.

Personal hot-issue alerts COMING SOON

An around-the-clock watch over the symbols in your RUNs. The key idea: a market event is a fact about the market — whether it matters is a fact about you. So every alert is verified against your own account before it's sent:

  1. Detect. Every hour, the platform scans all of your active symbols for real changes — policy news, breaking headlines, sharp technical moves, unusual crowd sentiment.
  2. Verify for you. A detected event is checked against your position: which direction you hold, your leverage, how far your safety stop is. The same news can be good for one member, bad for another, and irrelevant for a third.
  3. Alert — or stay silent. Only if it actually matters for your position do you get a personal alert, written for your situation (for example: "this policy news runs against your short — your stop is 1.1% away"). If it doesn't affect you, no notification. Silence is part of the product.
