What this means for you

US, Canadian and Australian hours shift by an hour when daylight saving starts or ends — and Australia's runs opposite to the northern hemisphere. The site always shows the current, correct time.

Setting your region

You choose your region and language when you first sign in, and can change either at any time in Settings → Region & Language. Your region sets the clock every time on the site is shown in.
