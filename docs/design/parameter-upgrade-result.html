⑤ Phase-4 evidence

The walk-forward backtest: honest scope, honest verdict

Anchored 4-fold walk-forward, 14-day purge, 2019→2026 (per-asset windows: BTC/ETH signals from 2020-02, SOL from 2021-05). B4 (crowd sentiment) has no historical archive and stayed null throughout — the backtested ≥4/6 gate is therefore stricter than the live one, which is exactly why only historically-evaluated conditions could ever earn default-on.

Pre-registered go/no-go — primary row (deployment-faithful config, never tuned)

  • ✕Evidence floor: 38 pooled OOS trades < 400 required. At this n, a zero-edge strategy passes a naive 52% accuracy bar ~1 time in 3 — which is precisely why the floor exists.
  • ✕Win-rate Wilson 90% lower bound > 0.50: observed 36.8% (14/38), lower bound 0.276.
  • ✕Profit-factor bootstrap 90% lower bound > 1.0: point estimate 1.13, lower bound 0.644 — profitability not distinguishable from break-even.
  • ✓Worst-asset max drawdown ≤ 30%: 2.18%. The risk stack is the part that works.
