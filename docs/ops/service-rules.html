1. Free trial — Junior, 7 days, no payment method

규칙 1 — 주니어 7일 무료, 결제수단 없이 (2026-08-05 소유자 확정)

Revised 2026-08-05. The owner reversed the original card-up-front design the same day it was written: "TRIAL is first 7 days don't need subscription payment (Stripe, PayPal)", and "after 7 days, stop all user daemon and services, email to user and always open Subscription page." This section now describes the card-free design — which is what is implemented (billing_sync.grant_trial, branch feat/trial-reminder).

2026-08-05 개정. 카드 선등록 방식은 같은 날 소유자 지시로 폐기되었습니다. 현재 구현된 것은 아래의 카드 없는 체험입니다.

The rule as customers read it

Junior — first 7 days free, no card. Every new account automatically receives 7 days of the Junior plan. No payment method is collected, and the trial never converts into a paid subscription: nothing is charged during the trial and nothing is charged when it ends. On day 8 your automation is paused, you are emailed, and the subscribe page opens; access resumes when you choose and pay for a plan.

The free trial is offered once per account, on the Junior plan only. Pro and Professional bill from day one. Subscribing to a paid plan during the trial ends the trial and begins the paid period immediately.

주니어 첫 7일 무료 — 카드 불필요. 새 계정은 가입 즉시 주니어 7일 체험을 자동으로 받습니다. 결제수단을 수집하지 않으며, 체험은 유료 구독으로 전환되지 않습니다 — 체험 중에도, 종료 시에도 청구가 없습니다. 8일째에 자동화가 일시정지되고 이메일 안내 후 구독 페이지가 열립니다. 무료 체험은 계정당 1회, 주니어 플랜에만 적용됩니다. 체험 중 유료 플랜을 구독하면 체험이 종료되고 즉시 유료 기간이 시작됩니다.

What bounds the cost of a card-free trial

A no-card trial is a bill with no floor only if it has no hard stop. This one stops itself: the trial is an ordinary subscription row that expires on day 7, at which point PLAN_LIMITS[None] zeroes the member's run allowance and the subscription enforcer pauses every daemon they own. Seven days of Junior-tier LLM/data quota per account is the maximum spend, and the day-4 reminder plus expiry notice are sent by the same sweep that pauses the runs.

카드 없는 체험의 비용은 7일에서 강제로 끝납니다. 체험은 7일째 만료되는 일반 구독 행이며, 만료 즉시 실행 한도가 0이 되어 모든 데몬이 일시정지됩니다. 계정당 최대 지출은 주니어 등급 7일치 쿼터입니다.

One trial per account — the enforcement question

With no card there is no payment fingerprint, so the guard keys on the account's subscription history: any prior subscription row — active, expired, or superseded — blocks a new trial (billing_sync.has_prior_subscription). A lapsed trial therefore never earns a second one. Email aliasing can still mint fresh accounts; verified phone (shipped in #242) is the strong key to add if repeated-account abuse actually shows up. Watch it, don't pre-build it.

카드가 없으므로 카드 지문 대신 계정의 구독 이력이 기준입니다 — 어떤 상태든 구독 행이 하나라도 있으면 새 체험이 부여되지 않습니다. 반복 가입 남용이 실제로 관측되면 이미 구현된 휴대폰 인증을 키로 추가하십시오.

2. No refunds, no proration

규칙 2 — 환불 없음, 일할 계산 없음

The rule as customers read it

All subscription fees are non-refundable, and unused time is not pro-rated.

Sooppiy is not a passive content service. From the moment a billing period begins, the platform spends real money on your behalf and cannot recover it: paid AI model inference for your research, decisions and verifications; licensed market, news and sentiment data feeds; and continuous compute for your automation runs, monitoring and alerting. Those costs are consumed as you use the service and are not returned to us if you stop.

Accordingly:

  • No refunds are given for a billing period that has started — whole or partial, used or unused.
  • Cancelling mid-period does not credit, refund or pro-rate the remaining days. Your access simply continues until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Downgrading takes effect at the next billing cycle. The difference for the current period is not refunded.
  • Not using the service during a period is not grounds for a refund.

The 7-day Junior free trial exists so you can evaluate the platform fully before any charge is made. Use it.

구독료는 환불되지 않으며, 남은 기간은 일할 계산되지 않습니다. 결제 주기가 시작되는 순간부터 회원님을 위해 유료 AI 추론, 라이선스 시장·뉴스·감성 데이터, 자동화 실행과 모니터링 연산에 실제 비용이 지출되며 회수되지 않습니다. 따라서 시작된 주기에 대한 환불(전부·일부, 사용 여부 무관)은 없고, 중도 해지 시에도 남은 일수는 환급·크레딧·일할 처리되지 않으며 결제한 기간 끝까지 이용만 유지됩니다. 하향 변경은 다음 주기부터 적용되고 차액은 환불되지 않습니다. 미사용은 환불 사유가 아닙니다. 7일 무료 체험이 청구 전 평가 수단입니다.

Exceptions you should still keep operational, not legal advice

A blanket "never, under any circumstance" invites chargebacks, and a chargeback costs you the disputed amount plus a fee and dispute-rate damage. Keep a narrow, discretionary carve-out and say so — it is cheaper than the disputes it prevents:

  • Duplicate or clearly erroneous charges — always refunded.
  • Extended platform outage that made the service unusable for a material part of the period.
  • Fraudulent use of a stolen card — refund and close the account.

"어떤 경우에도 없음"은 지불거절(chargeback)을 부릅니다. 지불거절은 금액에 더해 수수료와 분쟁률 손해까지 발생하므로, 중복 청구·장기 장애·도용 카드에 한한 좁은 예외를 명시해 두는 편이 더 쌉니다.

Jurisdiction check — flagging, not advising. A no-refund term does not automatically override mandatory consumer-withdrawal rights. Korea's 전자상거래법 and the EU/UK distance-selling rules both grant a statutory cooling-off window, with an exception for digital services the consumer expressly agreed to begin consuming immediately. Two things follow, and both are worth doing regardless:
  1. The 7-day free trial already functions as a cooling-off period, which is the strongest practical answer to this objection — a customer is charged only after a full week of free evaluation.
  2. At checkout, have the customer explicitly consent to immediate commencement of the digital service and acknowledge the resulting loss of withdrawal right. That consent is what the statutory exception turns on, and it needs to be a recorded checkbox, not a line buried in the terms.

Have a lawyer in your billing jurisdiction confirm the final wording. I am flagging a known risk, not giving legal advice.

환불 불가 조항이 강행 규정인 청약철회권을 자동으로 배제하지는 않습니다. 다만 ① 7일 무료 체험 자체가 사실상 숙려기간 역할을 하고, ② 결제 시 "즉시 이용 개시 및 청약철회권 상실"에 대한 명시적 동의 체크박스를 받아 기록하면 법정 예외 요건에 부합합니다. 최종 문구는 반드시 변호사 확인을 받으십시오. 법률 자문이 아니라 알려진 리스크 고지입니다.

3.2 Cancel any time

You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal — it does not end the period you have already paid for. Access, automation runs and alerts continue until expires_at, then the account returns to unsubscribed and the platform locks.
  • Card (Stripe): Manage subscription → the Stripe Customer Portal → cancel.
  • PayPal: Manage subscription → Cancel auto-renewal.
  • USDT / bank transfer: nothing renews by itself — the period simply ends unless a new payment is made.
  • During the free trial: nothing to cancel — no payment method is on file and the trial simply ends on day 7.

언제든 해지할 수 있습니다. 해지는 다음 갱신을 중지할 뿐, 이미 결제한 기간을 종료시키지 않습니다. expires_at까지 이용·자동화·알림이 유지된 뒤 잠금 상태가 됩니다. USDT·계좌이체는 자동 갱신이 없어 기간이 끝나면 그대로 종료됩니다. 체험 기간에는 등록된 결제수단이 없으므로 해지할 것도 없습니다 — 7일째에 저절로 끝납니다.

3.3 Back up your activity before you cancel customer obligation

Export your data before your access ends. This is your responsibility, and it cannot be undone for you.

Your trading history, run journals, decision logs, positions, P&L records and research outputs are readable only while your subscription is active. Once access ends:

  • You can no longer open or export them yourself.
  • High-resolution run data is permanently deleted on a rolling schedule and cannot be reconstructed — not by support, not by re-subscribing. Equity time-series is pruned after 7 days regardless of subscription state.
  • Re-subscribing later restores your account, but not data that has already aged out.

Use Export my data before you cancel. If you skip it, the loss is permanent and we cannot recover it for you.

해지 전에 데이터를 내보내십시오. 이는 회원 책임이며 대신 복구해 드릴 수 없습니다. 거래 내역·실행 저널·판단 로그·포지션·손익·리서치 결과는 구독이 활성일 때만 열람·내보내기가 가능합니다. 접근이 종료되면 직접 내보낼 수 없고, 고해상도 실행 데이터는 주기적으로 영구 삭제되어 재구독해도 복구되지 않습니다(에쿼티 시계열은 구독 상태와 무관하게 7일 후 삭제). 건너뛰면 손실은 영구적입니다.

This rule is not yet enforceable, and stating it before it is would be unfair. There is no "Export my data" feature in the product today — /api/research/export/* exports challenge and team datasets, not a member's own trading activity. Telling customers to back up while giving them no way to do it converts a reasonable rule into a complaint, and a chargeback argument. Ship the export first (§5.3), publish the rule with it.

현재 제품에 "내 데이터 내보내기" 기능이 없습니다(/api/research/export/*는 챌린지·팀 데이터셋이지 회원 개인 거래 활동이 아닙니다). 방법을 주지 않고 백업하라고 고지하면 정당한 규칙이 민원과 지불거절 사유가 됩니다. 내보내기 기능을 먼저 출시한 뒤 이 규칙을 공개하십시오.

5. What has to be built

5.1 Trial support built 2026-08-05 — card-free, platform-side

Superseded note (2026-08-05): the provider-native trial described below was never built. The owner's card-free reversal made it unnecessary — the shipped design (branch feat/trial-reminder) is entirely platform-side: billing_sync.grant_trial() inserts an ordinary active Junior row (method='trial', auto_renew=0, expires_at=+7d) at both signup paths; lazy expiry plus PLAN_LIMITS[None] pause every daemon on day 8; the day-4 reminder and expiry notice ride subscription_enforcer_loop; once-per-account is enforced by has_prior_subscription(). Per §2's jurisdiction note: a recorded checkbox for immediate commencement of the digital service and acknowledgement of the no-refund term. Store the timestamp and the terms version with the subscription row — an unrecorded consent is no consent when a dispute arrives.

5.3 "Export my data" does not exist

Rule 3.3 is unfair until this ships. Scope it to what a member would actually miss: positions, closed trades with P&L, run history and run events, decision/journal logs, and research theses — CSV or JSON, scoped to the requesting user, downloadable while the subscription is active.
