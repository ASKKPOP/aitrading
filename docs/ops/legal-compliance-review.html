Verdict first

결론 요약

  1. Two statements on the live site are now false and get worse the moment you charge — Privacy Policy §2 ("the Service has no paid features at this time") and the old Terms §8, which I already rewrote. The privacy one is still live.
  2. The biggest risk in this review is not the documents — it is financial-services licensing. Charging a fee for trading signals and running automation against a customer's brokerage account is a regulated activity in all eight markets under different names. No amount of policy wording fixes that. This needs a licensing opinion before you take money from any of these countries.
  3. "No refunds" is not lawful as an absolute in several of these markets — in Australia a flat "no refunds" statement is itself an offence under the ACL, independent of whether you would have refunded.
  4. The arbitration + class-waiver + California-law clause (Terms §15) is unenforceable against consumers in several target markets and is a liability in itself.
  5. No legal entity is identified anywhere — no company name, registration number, or address. Korea and Japan make that disclosure mandatory to sell to consumers at all, and Stripe will ask for it too.

① 라이브 사이트에 거짓 문구 2개(개인정보처리방침 2조는 아직 수정 전). ② 이 검토에서 가장 큰 리스크는 문서가 아니라 금융업 인허가입니다 — 8개국 모두에서 규제 대상 행위이며 문구로 해결되지 않습니다. ③ "환불 없음"의 절대적 표현은 위법인 시장이 있습니다(호주는 표시 자체가 위반). ④ 중재·집단소송 포기·캘리포니아법 조항은 여러 시장에서 소비자 상대로 집행 불가. ⑤ 법인 정보가 어디에도 없습니다 — 한국·일본은 이 표시가 판매의 법정 요건입니다.

Part C — The licensing question read this one

C부 — 금융업 인허가: 이 검토에서 가장 큰 리스크

You charge a fee. In exchange, an AI produces trading signals and decisions for an identified individual user, and — where the user connects a broker — places orders on that user's account. In every one of these eight markets, some part of that is a regulated activity. The disclaimer "nothing here is investment advice" is evidence, not a defence: regulators look at what the service does, not at what it calls itself.

Two features push you toward the heavier end of every regime: the advice is personalised (per user, per portfolio, per risk setting) rather than an impersonal newsletter, and the system can execute rather than merely suggest.

수수료를 받고, 특정 개인 사용자를 위해 AI가 매매 신호·판단을 생성하며, 브로커 연결 시 그 계좌에 주문까지 넣습니다. 8개국 모두에서 이 중 일부는 규제 대상 행위입니다. "투자 자문이 아님" 문구는 방어가 아니라 정황 증거일 뿐이며, 규제기관은 명칭이 아니라 실제 기능을 봅니다. 특히 ① 자문이 개인 맞춤형이고 ② 시스템이 주문 집행까지 한다는 두 가지가 가장 무거운 쪽으로 분류시킵니다.

The pragmatic path most platforms take, and the one worth pricing before you build compliance for eight countries: geo-restrict at signup to the markets where you have an opinion, and open others as opinions come in. That is a billing-country check plus a terms clause, and it is far cheaper than eight licensing analyses done at once. It also matches what your payment providers will expect you to be able to state.

현실적인 경로: 의견서를 확보한 시장만 열고 나머지는 가입 단계에서 지역 제한한 뒤 순차 개방. 결제 국가 확인 + 약관 조항이면 되며, 8개국 인허가 검토를 한꺼번에 하는 것보다 훨씬 저렴합니다.

Honest limits of this review

  • I audited documents against requirements. I did not and cannot assess how you actually process data in practice, which is what an audit under any of these statutes really examines.
  • Statute names and obligations are stated to the best of my knowledge as of May 2026. Privacy and consumer law in Vietnam, Indonesia and Australia were all mid-transition then; treat every verify as a research task, not a finding.
  • I have deliberately not drafted replacement legal text for items 7–12. Wrong legal drafting that reads convincingly is more dangerous than an obvious gap.
  • Thresholds matter and I do not know your numbers — CCPA, the Australian small-business exemption, and several DPO duties turn on revenue or data volume.
